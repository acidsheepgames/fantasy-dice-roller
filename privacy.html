Privacy Policy

For the app Fantasy Dice Roller on iOS and Android and for this website. Effective 14 November 2026. Deutsche Fassung

In short: the app has no account, no ads and no advertising tracking. Your dice, presets and pictures stay on your phone. Purchases go through Apple or Google. The Unity software the app is built with sends some technical data to Unity.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your settings, presets, roll history, custom dice and the pictures you put on them are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. I have no access to them and they are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

4. Purchases

The Pro upgrade and the tips are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. I never see your name, e-mail address or payment details; I only receive sales reports without personal data. The app keeps a note on your phone that Pro is unlocked.

5. Unity

The app is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's services for in-app purchases and basic analytics, provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA, and its affiliates). When the app runs, these components can send technical data to Unity:

  • device model, operating system version, app version, language and screen size
  • an installation or device identifier, on iPhone the identifier for the app vendor (IDFV); never your advertising ID
  • your IP address, as with every internet connection, from which a rough country can be derived
  • technical events such as app start, session length and purchase events

I use this to see how many people use which app version and to keep purchases working. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a working and maintained app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for purchases, the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I do not combine this data with other data and do not use it for advertising.

Unity may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. Details: Unity Privacy Policy.

6. No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. Data is not sold and not shared with data brokers.

7. If you write to me

If you send an e-mail, I use your address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest after three years.

8. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). As with every website, the host processes your IP address and browser details in server logs to deliver the page and for security. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to acidsheepgames@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, I cannot look up data by your name or e-mail address.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
